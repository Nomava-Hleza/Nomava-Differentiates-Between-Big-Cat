Can You Tell a Leopard, Cheetah and Jaguar Apart?

Three magnificent cats. Three distinctive patterns. One fascinating difference.

Spot the Difference!

At first glance, their coats might look almost identical. We've all seen their distinctive markings, but could you confidently tell which belongs to a leopard, a cheetah or a jaguar?

The secret lies in the details. What makes these three magnificent predators look so similar is precisely what makes them different: their spots, rosettes and unique markings.

Let's take a closer look.

The very thing that makes these animals look alike is also what sets them apart: their SPOTS!

The Leopard

Facts about Leopards

  1. They have rosette-shaped markings.
  2. They are excellent climbers.
  3. They often carry their prey into trees.

The Jaguar

Facts about Jaguars

  • They have large rosettes, often with spots inside.
  • They are powerful swimmers.
  • They are the largest wild cats in the Americas.

The Cheetah

Facts about Cheetahs

  1. They have solid black spots rather than rosettes.
  2. They have distinctive black tear marks on their faces.
  3. They are the fastest land animals.
